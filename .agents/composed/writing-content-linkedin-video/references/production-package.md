# Production package

Create only the artifacts the selected production scope needs. Keep filenames
stable so the work can move from planning to recording or editing.

## `brief.md`

Capture:

* Audience, situation, and intended response
* One-sentence promise
* Central claim and supporting evidence
* Source links and supplied assets
* Author voice and words to avoid
* Call to action
* Target length, framing, and deadline
* Assumptions, permissions, and unresolved risks

## `script.md`

Write short, time-coded beats for speech:

* **Hook** - Give immediate context, tension, or a useful surprise.
* **Setup** - Name the problem in the audience's language.
* **Proof** - Show an artifact, demonstration, or concrete example.
* **Payoff** - State what the viewer can now understand or do.
* **Call to action** - Invite one relevant step without engagement bait.

Separate spoken words, on-screen text, and visual direction. Read the script
aloud and cut phrases that sound written rather than spoken.

## `shot-list.md`

Describe each shot with:

* Approximate time range
* Subject, framing, and action
* Spoken line or audio cue
* On-screen text
* Purpose in the story
* Required asset and source
* Transition into the next shot

Make the first visual useful before narration begins. Mark shots that depend on
an unverified claim, unavailable asset, or future recording.

## Captions

Provide `captions.srt` when reliable timestamps exist. Otherwise provide
`caption-transcript.md` with clean sentence breaks. Never fabricate timestamps
from a script that has not been recorded.

## `post.md`

Write companion copy that:

* Adds context instead of transcribing the video
* Makes the professional relevance clear in the opening
* Supports factual claims with links when needed
* Uses spacing that scans well in the feed
* Ends with one genuine question or next step when useful
* Uses only relevant mentions and hashtags

Never invent engagement hooks, controversy, personal history, or endorsement.

## `qa.md`

Record:

* Claim, source, and author voice review
* Opening clarity without sound
* Text readability, contrast, and safe zones
* Caption accuracy and timing
* Audio intelligibility and level consistency
* Export container, codecs, duration, dimensions, frame rate, and bitrate
* Representative frame and playback review
* LinkedIn requirement source and check date
* Remaining recording or publishing action
